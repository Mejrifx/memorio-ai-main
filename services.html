How the handoff works, in detail

For the director who wants to see the whole pipeline before trusting it with a family. This page walks through every stage of a Memorio tribute, what your team touches, what we handle, and how family information is protected.

Start with a free tribute See pricing

One tribute, five stages

1

Your staff opens a new tribute

Enter the basic information, open the tribute in your dashboard, and hand the family their secure link. That is your team's entire involvement in production.

Time required: about two minutes.

2

The family shares, from home

Through their secure link, the family answers compassionate, guided questions and uploads cherished photos at their own pace, in English or Spanish. The experience is built for people who may not be comfortable with technology, during a difficult time.

No follow-up calls from your staff. Support is available to the family throughout.

3

The obituary is written

The moment the family completes their form, Memorio drafts the obituary from their own words, memories, and details. It is a draft, not a final: it goes to human review like everything else.

Drafted immediately on form completion.

4

The tribute video is edited and reviewed

The video is assembled from the family's photos, memories, and music preferences: sequencing, pacing, and music chosen to honor a life with dignity. A person then reviews every word of the obituary and every frame of the video before anything is delivered.

Human quality review on every tribute, no exceptions.

5

Delivery, within 48 hours

The finished obituary and tribute video are delivered simultaneously to the family and your funeral home within 48 hours of submission, ready for services or online sharing.

A standardized pipeline your team can set expectations by, and meet.

What comes back

Every delivered tribute meets the same professional standard, whatever your week looks like. Nothing to assemble, nothing to fix, nothing waiting on a staff member's spare hour.

The written obituary

Built from the family's own memories and details, reviewed by a person, ready for programs, publication, and online sharing.

The tribute video

The family's photos and memories, edited with music and pacing that honor their loved one, checked frame by frame before delivery, ready for the service.

Built for sensitive information

Memorial work means holding a family's most personal material. Memorio is designed around that responsibility.

Strict access control

Only the right people can see each tribute. Family information is never exposed, shared, or mixed between accounts.

Secure storage and transmission

All photos, information, and memorial materials are stored and transmitted using modern security practices, with access controls, authentication, and monitoring.

Never sold, never advertised with

Personal information is not sold, and it is not shared for advertising purposes. Family material exists to create the tribute, nothing else.

The full detail lives in our privacy policy.
