The Two-Minute Handoff

Most tribute software still leaves the production with your staff: chasing photos, drafting obituaries, assembling videos, fielding revisions. Memorio is not software your team operates. It is a handoff.

Your team's part

  1. Enter the basic information.
  2. Open a new tribute.
  3. Hand the family their secure link.

That is the whole job. About two minutes.

Ours

  • Guide the family through photos and memoriesA gentle, step-by-step experience at home, in English or Spanish. No technical skills needed.
  • Write the obituaryDrafted the moment the family finishes, from their own words and memories.
  • Edit the tribute videoPhotos, music, sequencing, and pacing that honor a life with dignity.
  • Review everything by handA person checks every word and every frame before a family ever sees them.
  • Deliver within 48 hoursThe finished obituary and tribute video arrive to the family and your team at the same time.

A person reviews every tribute before it leaves

Software alone should never have the last word on someone's life story. At Memorio, the obituary begins as a careful draft built from the family's own memories, and the video is edited for pacing and feeling. Then a person reads every word and watches every frame before delivery.

Who actually creates the tribute?
Memorio drafts the obituary from what the family shares, then assembles the tribute video from their photos, memories, and music preferences. Every tribute passes through human quality review before final delivery.
48

Delivered within 48 hours

The obituary draft arrives the moment the family finishes their form. The finished tribute video follows within 48 hours of submission, ready for the service or online sharing. No bottlenecks, whatever the week looks like.

The same standard, every time

Every family receives a polished obituary and tribute video that meets the same standard, regardless of staff workload or schedule. Family information stays protected behind strict access controls, and is never shared or used for advertising.

Is the family's information safe?
Yes. Memories and personal information are protected with strong security measures and strict access controls. Nothing is mixed between accounts.

The first one is free

Because you can't judge memorial work from a brochure. Run Memorio with a real family, look at what comes back, and decide with the finished tribute in front of you. Then take 15 days to trial it across your services.

After that it stays simple: $150 per delivered tribute, billed monthly. No setup fees and no minimums. If a month brings no services, you pay nothing.

What does the $150 include?
Everything: the guided family experience in English or Spanish, the written obituary, the edited tribute video, human review, and delivery to the family and your team within 48 hours.
Will it be hard on families?
No. The process is gentle, guided, and built for people who may not be comfortable with technology, during a difficult time. Support is available every step of the way.
